Four rules
1. English first. Ittro starts in English. Arabic and French are one spoken command away.
2. Voice first, no screen. Every action is a spoken command, the language too. The earbud button and a long press on volume-down back up yes, no and stop.
3. Every word on screen is spoken. The screen shows only what Ittro says, the moment it says it. Command chips repeat words from the spoken line.
4. Never silent, never chatty. Words come only when something changes. Between them, ticks and vibration: never more than 2 s without a sound.
Silence budget: reading a shelf that takes 14 s
Waiting for the shopper
After a question, Ittro listens. With no answer, it asks again at 8 s, offers help at 20 s, then says a short reminder every 60 s. Saying stop silences it until the shopper says Ittro.
As fast as possible
Ittro starts looking while it asks "Is that right?", reads a product as soon as a fingertip rests on it, and plays direction words from recorded clips. Targets: first outline under 3 s after "yes", the right product in hand under 30 s.
